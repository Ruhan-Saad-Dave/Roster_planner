📘 LocalRoster Manager Reference & Feature Guide Interactive Guide

How to Build Your Monthly Roster in 3 Steps

LocalRoster uses Google's CP-SAT constraint optimization engine to generate mathematical schedules in seconds while respecting shift ergonomics and cross-department backup coverage.

1
Step 1: Check Your Organization & Pre-Fill Known Requests

Click ⚙️ Manage Org to add or edit departments and team members. Assign colors to make departments visually identifiable. If employees requested vacations or specific shifts, switch on Prefill Mode, click their calendar cell, set the shift or OFF day, and click Pin / Lock Shift 📌.

2
Step 2: Generate Roster with CP-SAT Engine

Click + New Roster, pick your target Year and Month, and select 3 Variants. Click Start CP-SAT Solve. In $\le 2$ seconds, the solver calculates the optimal schedule, balancing weekend duties, night shifts, and primary vs backup floating ranks.

3
Step 3: Inspect Roster Health & Export

Look at the top Traffic-Light Health Banner. If any shortage occurs, click the 1-Click Fix button to immediately resolve it. Switch to the Workload Audit Scorecard to confirm total hours. When ready, click Export Excel or Export PDF to print or distribute.

Traffic-Light Health Signals & 1-Click Fixes

The banner at the top continuously monitors schedule feasibility and labor health:

🟢 ALL CLEAR (Green)

Every single shift across all departments has met or exceeded minimum required staffing. Zero ergonomic rest violations exist.

🟡 ADVISORY / PARTIAL (Yellow)

Minimum coverage is achieved, but optional secondary preferences or optimal float targets could be improved.

🔴 SHORTAGES DETECTED (Red)

A department dips below minimum coverage due to multiple simultaneous leaves or headcount shortages. The banner provides a 1-Click Fix button that intelligently finds eligible backup staff with valid rest hours to fill the gap.

Ergonomic Labor Constraints Enforced by Solver

LocalRoster enforces strict mathematical invariants to protect employee health:

  • Mandatory Rest: At least 2 full rest days off per rolling 7-day window.
  • Max Consecutive Days: Ceiling of at most 5 consecutive shifts before mandatory rest is triggered.
  • Forward-Only Rotation: Inter-shift rest of $\ge 14$ hours. Working a Night Shift strictly forbids rotating directly into Morning or Evening shift on the following day.
  • Single Shift Per Day: An employee cannot work overlapping shifts or be booked twice on the same calendar day.
  • Multi-Skilled Floating Hierarchy: Primary department workers are scheduled first. If a shortage occurs, Rank 2 backup floaters are pulled before Rank 3 emergency floaters.

Month-to-Month Carryover & Continuous Scheduling

Schedules don't exist in isolation. When transitioning from one month to the next:

1. Toggle the 📅 Prev Month button on the header to display the final 3 days of the previous month right beside the employee names.

2. See total hours and night shifts worked in the previous month to ensure fair rotation across consecutive months.

3. If an employee worked a Night Shift on the final day of the previous month, LocalRoster alerts you if they are assigned to Morning on Day 1, ensuring no cross-month turnaround fatigue.
